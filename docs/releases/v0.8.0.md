# Coworker v0.8.0

See what you're choosing. When you compare places to stay, venues, or products, coworkers now show photos from their web search right beside each result.

## Photos in search results

- Coworkers add photos when seeing the subject helps you decide, such as Airbnb listings, hotels, venues, restaurants, and products, or whenever you ask for pictures ("show me photos of…", "include photos").
- Photos appear beside the result they belong to, in a table row or list item. When a coworker finds several photos of one option, they show as a small stack you can open.
- Photos that don't belong to a particular result, such as several views of one landmark, appear as a collage above the reply.
- Photos appear while the reply is still being written.
- Answers that are about facts, news, code, or policies stay text-only. Photos are also skipped for replies sent to Telegram or Discord, for files and emails, and for sensitive subjects such as medical conditions.

## Photo viewer

- Click a photo to zoom it open. Each photo shows its title and source above it.
- Slide between photos with the arrows beside the photo, the arrow keys, or the dots below. Press Esc or click outside to close.
- Opening a photo from a result shows only that result's photos.

## Safety

- Coworkers can only show images that their own web search returned during the same request.
- The app downloads each image itself, over public HTTPS only, and checks its size and format before showing it. The chat window never loads images directly from the web.
- Each attached image is recorded in the app's audit log.

## Interface

- Chat replies are wider, and table columns no longer squeeze names into a narrow strip.
- The README is shorter, with refreshed screenshots.
