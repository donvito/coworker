# Coworker v0.7.1

Get notified about new app releases and crop coworker photos before saving them.

## App update notices

- Coworker checks for a newer stable release in the background when it starts. Startup checks stay quiet when you are offline.
- An update modal offers **Download** to open the GitHub release page and **Later** to dismiss the notice for the current app session. Download and install the new version manually.
- A download icon at the bottom of the workspace and conversation sidebars stays visible while an update is available. Click it to reopen the modal.
- **Check for Updates** is in the **Coworker** menu on macOS and **Help** on Windows and Linux. Checking progress, the current-version result, and errors appear in a modal without moving your page.

## Coworker photos and settings

- Upload a photo, drag and zoom to position it, and check the circular preview before applying the crop.
- In coworker settings, **Apply crop** and **Remove photo** save immediately, without scrolling to **Save changes**.
- Changing only a coworker's photo or avatar keeps its work and browser session running.
- Coworker settings has a close icon at the top right and a single photo preview beside its controls.
